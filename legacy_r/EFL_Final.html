2. Structural Analysis of Shot Accuracy (Cloud Investigation)

Raw Shot_Accuracy exhibits a distinct bimodal distribution divided into two separate “clouds” (Lower Cloud vs. Higher Cloud) due to historical shifts in data collection criteria across eras. Rather than standardizing the variable, we analyze the two regimes explicitly using raw values.

Key empirical findings from the baseline linear model (model_gd) include:

  • High Explanatory Power: The model exhibits a robust goodness-of-fit with an Adjusted \(R^2\) of \(0.9013\) and a residual standard error of \(4.585\) points.

  • Dominance of Goal Difference (GD): Serves as the primary driver of point accumulation (\(\hat{\beta} = 0.6973\), \(t = 44.629\), \(p < 0.001\)). Holding other variables constant, a \(+10\) improvement in GD corresponds to approximately \(+7\) additional total points.

  • Insignificance of Auxiliary Metrics: Neither raw Shot_Accuracy (\(p = 0.810\)) nor Net_SOT (\(p = 0.448\)) adds statistically significant explanatory value in the presence of GD, as goal difference absorbs the downstream impact of shooting performance.

  • Irrelevance of Discipline: Disciplinary penalty points (Card_Points, \(p = 0.738\)) do not exert a statistically meaningful effect on final point totals.

7. Final Baseline Model Selection Conclusion

The formal diagnostic evaluation and comparative model assessment culminate in the selection of model_gd as the definitive baseline OLS specification for predicting EFL Championship point totals.

Summary of Model Selection Criteria

  • Explanatory Capability: Both model specifications demonstrate exceptional explanatory power, accounting for over \(90.2\%\) of the variance in total points accumulated (\(R^2 \approx 0.9024\), Adjusted \(R^2 \approx 0.9016\)).

  • Parsimony and Information Criteria: model_gd achieves a lower Akaike Information Criterion (\(\text{AIC} = 2829.58\)) compared to the decomposed specification (model_gf_ga). By consolidating goal scoring and conceding metrics into net Goal Difference (\(\text{GD}\)), the model achieves greater structural parsimony without sacrificing goodness-of-fit.

  • Multicollinearity Elimination: Isolating \(\text{GD}\) eliminates the exact linear identity (\(\text{GD} = \text{GF} - \text{GA}\)) that induces structural singularity, reducing all variance inflation factors well below conservative thresholds (\(\text{VIF} \le 2.260\)).

  • Diagnostic Robustness: Diagnostic checks validate linear functional form (Box-Cox \(\hat{\lambda} \approx 0.82\), encompassing \(\lambda = 1.0\)), homoscedasticity (\(BP = 5.740, p = 0.2194\)), and residual distribution stability (\(W = 0.990\)). Identified leverage points (e.g., Fulham 2021/22) and exogenous anomalies (e.g., Derby County’s \(21\)-point penalty) exhibit minimal influence on coefficient estimates (\(\text{Cook's } D \le 0.063\)).

Consequently, model_gd—utilizing net Goal Difference (\(\text{GD}\)), Net Shots on Target (\(\text{Net\_SOT}\)), raw Shot Accuracy (\(\text{Shot\_Accuracy}\)), and Disciplinary Card Points (\(\text{Card\_Points}\))—is selected as the primary baseline linear model for all subsequent promotion probability simulations and policy interpretations.
